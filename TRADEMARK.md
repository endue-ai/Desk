The code in this repository is open source under Apache-2.0. The names and marks are not. Apache-2.0
(section 6) grants no rights to use trade names, trademarks or logos.

## What the marks are

"endue", "endue Desk", "Eris", "Dysnomia" and "Tin", together with the logos and illustrations in
`brand/`, are trademarks of Team endue.

## What you may do

- Refer to endue Desk truthfully, for example "a plugin for endue Desk" or "based on endue Desk".
- Use screenshots and the unmodified files in `brand/` in reviews, articles, talks and plugin listings,
  as long as it is clear that you are not endue.
- Build, run and modify the code for yourself or your organisation.

## What you may not do

- Name a fork, a derivative product or a service "endue", "Desk by endue" or anything confusingly similar.
- Use the endue or Eris logos, the Eris orb or the state illustrations to brand or decorate your product.
- Suggest that endue endorses, certifies or supports your product when it does not.

## Forks

A fork that is distributed to others must:

1. Choose a new product name and app identifier.
2. Replace everything in `brand/` with its own assets.
3. Point update, account and brain endpoints at its own services.
